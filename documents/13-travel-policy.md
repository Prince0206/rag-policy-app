# Business Travel Policy

**Document ID:** POL-013  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Finance / Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides guidelines for business travel undertaken by employees of Acme Corporation. It ensures that travel is conducted in a cost-effective, safe, and efficient manner while supporting business objectives.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who travel on behalf of Acme Corporation, whether domestic or international.

## 3. Travel Authorization

### 3.1 Pre-Approval

All business travel must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager before booking. International travel requires additional VP-level approval.

### 3.2 Travel Request Process

1. Submit a travel request via Concur at least 14 days before the planned departure.
2. Include: destination, purpose, dates, estimated costs, and attendees (if applicable).
3. Manager must approve or deny within 3 business days.
4. Once approved, book through Concur using the company's preferred vendors.

## 4. Booking Guidelines

### 4.1 Preferred Vendors

- Airlines: United, Delta, American (use corporate discount codes).
- Hotels: Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt (corporate rates available in Concur).
- Car Rental: Enterprise, National (corporate account).

### 4.2 Advance Booking

- Book flights at least 14 days in advance to secure lower fares.
- Last-minute bookings (under 7 days) require director-level approval.
- Always select the lowest reasonable fare within a 2-hour window of the preferred departure time.

### 4.3 Travel Class

| Trip Duration / Type       | Class Allowed        |
|---------------------------|---------------------|
| Domestic flights          | Economy             |
| International < 6 hours  | Economy             |
| International > 6 hours  | Premium Economy     |
| International > 10 hours | Business Class      |
| VP and above (any flight) | Business Class      |

Upgrades using personal frequent flyer miles are permitted.

## 5. During Travel

### 5.1 Safety

- Register all international trips with the company's travel security provider (International SOS).
- Download the Acme Travel Safety app for real-time alerts.
- Keep copies of important documents (passport, visa, insurance cards) in both physical and digital form.
- Share your itinerary with your manager and emergency contact.

### 5.2 Communication

- Employees are expected to remain reachable during business hours while traveling.
- Check in with your manager daily during extended trips.
- Adjust your Slack/Teams status to reflect your travel schedule.

### 5.3 Health and Wellness

- The company provides travel medical insurance for all business trips.
- For international travel, check vaccination requirements at least 30 days before departure.
- The company will reimburse required vaccinations and travel health consultations.

## 6. Expense Reporting

All travel expenses must be submitted through Concur within 14 days of trip completion. See the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-004) for detailed guidelines and per diem rates.

## 7. Frequent Flyer Programs

- Employees may keep personal frequent flyer miles and hotel points earned during business travel.
- However, employees must not select more expensive options solely to earn additional points/miles.
- The company does not maintain corporate loyalty accounts.

## 8. Travel Insurance

The company provides the following travel insurance coverage for all business trips:
- Medical emergency coverage: Up to $500,000.
- Emergency evacuation: Up to $250,000.
- Trip cancellation/interruption: Up to $5,000.
- Lost/delayed baggage: Up to $1,000.

Claims should be submitted through HR within 30 days of the incident.

## 9. Sustainability

Acme Corporation is committed to reducing our travel carbon footprint:
- Consider virtual meetings as an alternative to travel when possible.
- Use direct flights when available (fewer emissions).
- Combine multiple meetings into a single trip when feasible.
- The company purchases carbon offsets for all business air travel.

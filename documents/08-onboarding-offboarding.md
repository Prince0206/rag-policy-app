# Employee Onboarding and Offboarding Policy

**Document ID:** POL-008  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources / IT  

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the procedures for onboarding new employees and offboarding departing employees at Acme Corporation. A structured onboarding and offboarding process ensures a positive employee experience, protects company assets, and maintains security.

## 2. Onboarding

### 2.1 Pre-Arrival (1–2 weeks before start date)

HR and IT will complete the following before the new hire's first day:

- **HR Tasks:**
  - Send welcome email with first-day details (location, time, dress code, parking).
  - Prepare and send new hire paperwork (offer letter, tax forms, benefits enrollment, emergency contact).
  - Create employee profile in HRIS (Workday).
  - Assign a peer buddy from the same team.
  - Schedule orientation sessions.

- **IT Tasks:**
  - Provision laptop and peripherals.
  - Create email account and assign licenses (Google Workspace, Slack, Zoom).
  - Set up access to relevant systems based on role (Jira, GitHub, Salesforce, etc.).
  - Prepare security credentials (badge, VPN access).

- **Manager Tasks:**
  - Prepare a 30-60-90 day plan for the new hire.
  - Schedule introductory meetings with key team members and stakeholders.
  - Assign initial projects or learning tasks.

### 2.2 First Day

| Time          | Activity                                          |
|---------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| 9:00 AM       | Welcome and office tour (or virtual introduction) |
| 9:30 AM       | HR orientation: benefits, policies, culture       |
| 10:30 AM      | IT setup: laptop, accounts, security training     |
| 11:30 AM      | Meet the team lunch (company-sponsored)            |
| 1:00 PM       | Manager 1:1: role overview, expectations, 30-60-90 plan |
| 2:00 PM       | Buddy introduction and workspace setup             |
| 3:00 PM       | Self-paced: review policies, complete online training |

### 2.3 First Week

- Complete all mandatory training modules:
  - Security Awareness Training (2 hours)
  - Anti-Harassment Training (1 hour)
  - Code of Conduct Review (30 minutes)
  - Data Privacy Training (1 hour)
- Attend department overview presentations.
- Meet with buddy daily for questions and guidance.
- Complete ergonomic workspace self-assessment.

### 2.4 First 90 Days (Probationary Period)

- Weekly 1:1 meetings with manager.
- Complete all role-specific training.
- Receive and provide feedback at 30, 60, and 90-day marks.
- At 90 days: formal probationary review with manager and HR.
- Successful completion: full employee status confirmed.
- Unsuccessful: probation may be extended (up to 30 additional days) or employment may be terminated.

## 3. Offboarding

### 3.1 Voluntary Resignation

- **Notice Period:** Employees are expected to provide at least 2 weeks' written notice. Managers and directors are expected to provide 4 weeks' notice.
- **Resignation Letter:** Submit to direct manager and cc HR at hr@acmecorp.com.

### 3.2 Involuntary Termination

- All involuntary terminations must be approved by HR and Legal.
- A termination meeting is conducted with the employee, their manager, and an HR representative.
- Severance packages, if applicable, are outlined in the separation agreement.

### 3.3 Offboarding Checklist

**Employee's Last 2 Weeks:**

| Task                                        | Responsible |
|---------------------------------------------|-------------|
| Submit resignation letter                    | Employee    |
| Knowledge transfer documentation             | Employee    |
| Return company equipment (laptop, badge, keys)| Employee    |
| Complete exit interview                       | HR          |
| Process final paycheck and PTO payout         | Payroll     |
| Deactivate accounts and access              | IT          |
| Remove from distribution lists and channels  | IT          |
| Update org chart and team directories        | HR          |
| Provide references policy information        | HR          |
| Revoke building access                       | Facilities  |

### 3.4 Exit Interview

HR conducts an exit interview (in person or via video) to:
- Understand the employee's reasons for leaving.
- Gather feedback on management, culture, and processes.
- Identify areas for improvement.
- Ensure all offboarding tasks are complete.

Exit interview data is aggregated and reported to leadership quarterly (anonymized).

### 3.5 Account Deactivation Timeline

| System            | Deactivation Timing           |
|-------------------|------------------------------|
| Email             | End of last business day      |
| Slack/Teams       | End of last business day      |
| VPN               | End of last business day      |
| Building access   | End of last business day      |
| HRIS (Workday)    | Within 24 hours              |
| Code repositories | End of last business day      |
| Cloud storage     | After 30-day data retention   |

## 4. Equipment Return

All company equipment must be returned on or before the employee's last day. For remote employees:
- A prepaid shipping label will be provided.
- Equipment must be shipped within 3 business days of the last day.
- Failure to return equipment may result in deductions from the final paycheck (where permitted by law) or invoicing.

## 5. COBRA and Benefits Continuation

Departing employees will receive COBRA information within 14 days of their last day. COBRA allows continuation of health insurance coverage for up to 18 months at the employee's expense (plus a 2% administrative fee).

# Data Privacy and Protection Policy

**Document ID:** POL-012  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** February 15, 2025  
**Department:** Legal / Information Technology  

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the principles and requirements for the collection, use, storage, and protection of personal data at Acme Corporation. It ensures compliance with applicable data privacy laws and regulations, including GDPR, CCPA, and other relevant legislation.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who process personal data on behalf of Acme Corporation. It covers personal data of employees, customers, partners, and other individuals.

## 3. Definitions

- **Personal Data:** Any information that can identify a natural person, directly or indirectly (e.g., name, email, phone number, IP address).
- **Sensitive Personal Data:** Special categories of personal data requiring additional protection (e.g., health data, biometric data, racial/ethnic origin, religious beliefs).
- **Data Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, sharing, deletion).
- **Data Controller:** The entity that determines the purpose and means of processing (Acme Corporation).
- **Data Processor:** A third party that processes data on behalf of the controller.
- **Data Subject:** The individual whose personal data is being processed.

## 4. Data Privacy Principles

All data processing activities must adhere to these principles:

### 4.1 Lawfulness

Personal data must be processed based on a valid legal basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest).

### 4.2 Purpose Limitation

Data must be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and not processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes.

### 4.3 Data Minimization

Only the minimum amount of personal data necessary for the stated purpose should be collected and processed.

### 4.4 Accuracy

Personal data must be kept accurate and up to date. Inaccurate data must be corrected or deleted promptly.

### 4.5 Storage Limitation

Personal data must not be kept longer than necessary for the purposes for which it is processed. Refer to the Data Retention Schedule for specific timelines.

### 4.6 Integrity and Confidentiality

Personal data must be processed securely, with appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or damage.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Acme Corporation respects and facilitates the following rights of data subjects:

- **Right to Access:** Individuals may request a copy of their personal data.
- **Right to Rectification:** Individuals may request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to Erasure:** Individuals may request deletion of their data (subject to legal retention requirements).
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Individuals may request limitation of processing in certain circumstances.
- **Right to Data Portability:** Individuals may request their data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object:** Individuals may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Individuals may withdraw consent at any time.

Data subject requests must be fulfilled within 30 days. Requests should be directed to privacy@acmecorp.com.

## 6. Data Collection

### 6.1 Employee Data

The company collects the following categories of employee data:
- Personal identification (name, address, date of birth, SSN)
- Employment details (position, salary, performance records)
- Benefits information (insurance selections, beneficiaries)
- IT usage data (login times, system access logs)

### 6.2 Customer Data

Customer data is collected only as necessary for service delivery and in accordance with the company's Privacy Notice. Customer data includes:
- Contact information (name, email, phone)
- Account and billing information
- Service usage data
- Support interaction records

### 6.3 Consent

Where consent is the legal basis for processing, it must be:
- Freely given
- Specific to the processing purpose
- Informed (clear explanation of what data is collected and why)
- Unambiguous (clear affirmative action)
- Documented and revocable

## 7. Data Sharing and Transfer

### 7.1 Internal Sharing

Personal data may be shared internally only on a need-to-know basis and in accordance with the principle of least privilege.

### 7.2 Third-Party Sharing

Personal data may only be shared with third parties when:
- A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is in place.
- The third party has adequate security measures.
- The sharing is necessary for a legitimate business purpose.
- The data subject has been informed (via the Privacy Notice).

### 7.3 International Transfers

Transfer of personal data outside the country of collection must comply with applicable cross-border transfer mechanisms (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions). All international transfers must be approved by the Legal department.

## 8. Data Retention

Personal data must be retained only for as long as necessary:

| Data Category              | Retention Period         |
|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| Employee records          | 7 years after separation |
| Payroll records           | 7 years                  |
| Recruitment records       | 2 years                  |
| Customer records          | 5 years after last activity |
| Email communications      | 7 years                  |
| CCTV footage              | 30 days                  |
| Website analytics         | 2 years                  |

Data past its retention period must be securely deleted or anonymized.

## 9. Data Breach Response

### 9.1 Definition

A data breach is any incident that results in unauthorized access to, disclosure of, or loss of personal data.

### 9.2 Response Procedure

1. **Detect:** Identify and contain the breach.
2. **Assess:** Determine the scope, type of data affected, and number of individuals impacted.
3. **Notify:** 
   - Internal: Notify the Privacy Officer and Security team within 1 hour.
   - Regulatory: Notify relevant authorities within 72 hours (where required by law).
   - Affected individuals: Notify without undue delay if the breach poses a high risk.
4. **Remediate:** Implement measures to prevent recurrence.
5. **Document:** Maintain a record of the breach and response actions.

# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Finance  

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides guidelines for the reimbursement of business-related expenses incurred by employees of Acme Corporation. The company will reimburse employees for reasonable and necessary expenses incurred in the course of performing their job duties.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who incur business-related expenses on behalf of the company. Independent contractors should refer to their contract terms for expense guidelines.

## 3. General Principles

- All expenses must be directly related to company business.
- Expenses must be reasonable and prudent.
- Original receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
- Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense.
- Pre-approval is required for expenses exceeding $500.

## 4. Travel Expenses

### 4.1 Air Travel

- Economy class is standard for all domestic flights.
- Business class is permitted for international flights over 6 hours.
- Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance when possible.
- Employees should use the company's preferred travel booking platform (Concur).

### 4.2 Ground Transportation

- Rental cars: Mid-size or smaller, booked through the company portal.
- Ride-sharing (Uber/Lyft): Permitted for business travel; use standard (non-luxury) options.
- Mileage reimbursement: $0.67 per mile for personal vehicle use.
- Parking: Reasonable parking fees are reimbursable with receipt.

### 4.3 Lodging

- Standard room at a business-class hotel (e.g., Marriott, Hilton).
- Maximum nightly rate: $200 (Tier 1 cities: $300).
- Tier 1 cities include: New York, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Chicago, Boston, Washington D.C., Seattle.
- Extended stays (5+ nights): Contact the travel team for negotiated rates.

### 4.4 Meals During Travel

Daily meal allowances (per diem) for business travel:

| Meal      | Standard | Tier 1 Cities |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Breakfast | $20      | $25           |
| Lunch     | $25      | $35           |
| Dinner    | $40      | $55           |
| **Total** | **$85**  | **$115**      |

Alcohol is not reimbursable. Tips up to 20% are included in the meal limit.

## 5. Client Entertainment

### 5.1 Limits

- Client meals: Up to $100 per person.
- Client events/outings: Up to $200 per person; requires director-level pre-approval.
- All client entertainment expenses must include: date, attendees, business purpose.

### 5.2 Prohibited Expenses

The company will not reimburse:
- Personal entertainment or recreation
- Fines or penalties (including parking/traffic tickets)
- Personal phone charges
- Clothing (unless safety equipment)
- Spouse/partner travel expenses (unless specifically invited by the company)

## 6. Professional Development

- Conference registration: Up to $2,000 per year (pre-approved by manager).
- Books and subscriptions: Up to $500 per year.
- Online courses/certifications: Up to $1,500 per year through the Learning & Development portal.
- Professional memberships: Reimbursable if directly related to job function.

## 7. Submission Process

### 7.1 How to Submit

1. Log into the expense management system (Concur).
2. Create a new expense report.
3. Attach scanned receipts for all expenses over $25.
4. Categorize each expense appropriately.
5. Add business justification for each line item.
6. Submit for manager approval.

### 7.2 Approval Workflow

| Expense Amount | Approval Required       |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Under $500    | Direct manager         |
| $500 – $2,000 | Director               |
| $2,000 – $5,000 | VP                  |
| Over $5,000   | CFO                    |

### 7.3 Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expense reports are processed within 10 business days and deposited directly into the employee's payroll account.

## 8. Corporate Credit Cards

### 8.1 Eligibility

Employees who regularly incur business expenses of $500+ per month may apply for a corporate credit card through the Finance department.

### 8.2 Usage

- Corporate cards are for business expenses only.
- Personal charges on corporate cards are strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.
- Monthly statements must be reconciled and submitted by the 5th of each month.

## 9. Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:
- Denial of reimbursement
- Revocation of corporate credit card privileges
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination

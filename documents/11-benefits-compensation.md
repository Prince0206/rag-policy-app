# Benefits and Compensation Policy

**Document ID:** POL-011  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources / Finance  

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the benefits and compensation programs available to eligible employees of Acme Corporation. Our total rewards package is designed to attract, retain, and motivate top talent.

## 2. Eligibility

Full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week are eligible for all benefits. Part-time employees working 20–29 hours per week are eligible for limited benefits as noted below.

## 3. Health Insurance

### 3.1 Medical Plans

Acme Corporation offers three medical plan options through Aetna:

| Plan                  | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Monthly Premium (Family) | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|-----------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|------------|-------------------|
| PPO Standard          | $150                      | $450                    | $1,000     | $5,000            |
| PPO Premium           | $250                      | $650                    | $500       | $3,000            |
| HDHP with HSA         | $75                       | $225                    | $2,000     | $6,000            |

The company pays approximately 80% of the total premium cost.

### 3.2 Dental Insurance

- Provider: Delta Dental
- Employee premium: $25/month (family: $75/month)
- Coverage: 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major
- Annual maximum: $2,000 per person

### 3.3 Vision Insurance

- Provider: VSP
- Employee premium: $10/month (family: $30/month)
- Coverage: Annual eye exam ($10 copay), frames/lenses every 12 months ($150 allowance)
- Contact lens allowance: $150/year

### 3.4 Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees enrolled in the HDHP are eligible for an HSA:
- Company contribution: $750/year (individual) or $1,500/year (family)
- Employee contribution limit (2025): $4,150 (individual) or $8,300 (family)
- Funds roll over year to year and are portable

## 4. Retirement Benefits

### 4.1 401(k) Plan

- Employees may contribute from 1% to 75% of pre-tax or Roth salary.
- Company match: 100% of the first 4% of salary, plus 50% of the next 2% (up to 5% total employer contribution).
- Vesting: Immediate vesting for employee contributions; employer match vests over 3 years (33% per year).
- Enrollment: Automatic enrollment at 6% contribution rate after 30 days of employment (employees may opt out or adjust).

### 4.2 Financial Planning

- Free access to a financial advisor through Fidelity (quarterly sessions available).
- Retirement planning workshops offered semi-annually.

## 5. Life and Disability Insurance

### 5.1 Life Insurance

- Basic life insurance: 2x annual salary (company-paid, up to $500,000).
- Supplemental life insurance: Available for purchase up to 5x annual salary.
- Accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D): Equal to basic life insurance amount.

### 5.2 Short-Term Disability

- Coverage: 60% of base salary for up to 26 weeks.
- Waiting period: 7 calendar days.
- Company-paid benefit.

### 5.3 Long-Term Disability

- Coverage: 60% of base salary (up to $10,000/month).
- Waiting period: 180 days.
- Company-paid benefit.

## 6. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Free, confidential support services available to all employees and their household members:
- Mental health counseling (up to 8 sessions per issue per year)
- Legal consultations
- Financial counseling
- Work-life balance resources
- Substance abuse support
- Provider: ComPsych GuidanceResources
- Available 24/7: 1-800-555-EAP1 or eap.acmecorp.com

## 7. Wellness Programs

### 7.1 Gym Membership Subsidy

- Monthly subsidy of $50 toward gym membership fees.
- Submit receipts through the benefits portal for reimbursement.

### 7.2 Wellness Challenges

Quarterly wellness challenges with prizes:
- Step challenges
- Hydration challenges
- Mindfulness challenges
- Team fitness competitions

### 7.3 On-Site Wellness

- Flu shots offered annually (free).
- On-site biometric screenings offered annually.
- Meditation/quiet room available in all offices.

## 8. Education Benefits

### 8.1 Tuition Reimbursement

- Up to $5,250 per calendar year for job-related courses (IRS tax-free limit).
- Courses must be from an accredited institution.
- Employee must receive a grade of B or better (or pass for pass/fail courses).
- Application must be submitted and approved before course begins.

### 8.2 Student Loan Assistance

- Monthly contribution of $100 toward eligible student loan payments.
- Available after 1 year of service.
- Maximum lifetime benefit: $10,000.

## 9. Commuter Benefits

- Pre-tax commuter benefits for transit passes and parking.
- Monthly limits: $315 for transit, $315 for parking (2025 IRS limits).
- Bicycle commuter reimbursement: Up to $50/month for maintenance and equipment.

## 10. Other Benefits

- **Employee discount program:** Discounts on electronics, travel, entertainment, and more through PerkSpot.
- **Pet insurance:** Voluntary pet insurance through Nationwide at group rates.
- **Legal services:** Voluntary group legal plan through MetLife Legal Plans.
- **Identity theft protection:** Company-paid identity theft monitoring through LifeLock.

## 11. Open Enrollment

Open enrollment for benefits changes occurs annually in November (typically November 1–30). Outside of open enrollment, changes may only be made within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth/adoption, divorce, loss of other coverage).

# Remote Work and Flexible Work Arrangements Policy

**Document ID:** POL-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** March 15, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the guidelines and expectations for remote work and flexible work arrangements at Acme Corporation. The company recognizes that flexible work arrangements can improve employee satisfaction, productivity, and work-life balance while supporting business continuity.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees whose job functions can be performed remotely. Certain roles requiring physical presence (e.g., facilities, lab technicians, front desk) are excluded.

## 3. Work Arrangement Types

### 3.1 Fully Remote

Employees work from home or an approved remote location 5 days per week. Requires VP-level approval and a signed Remote Work Agreement.

### 3.2 Hybrid

Employees split their time between the office and a remote location. The standard hybrid schedule requires a minimum of 2 days per week in the office (Tuesday and Thursday are designated in-office days).

### 3.3 Flexible Hours

Employees may adjust their start and end times within the approved flex window:
- **Core hours:** 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM (local time) — all employees must be available.
- **Flex window:** 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM (local time) — employees may start/end within this window.

## 4. Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for remote or hybrid work, employees must:
- Have completed their 90-day probationary period.
- Have a satisfactory or above performance rating in their most recent review.
- Have a suitable home workspace with reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps download).
- Not be on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

## 5. Remote Work Agreement

All remote and hybrid employees must sign a Remote Work Agreement that covers:
- Designated work location(s)
- Work schedule and availability hours
- Equipment and technology requirements
- Data security and confidentiality obligations
- Ergonomic workspace self-certification

## 6. Equipment and Expenses

### 6.1 Company-Provided Equipment

The company will provide:
- Laptop computer
- External monitor (upon request)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset for video calls

### 6.2 Home Office Stipend

Remote and hybrid employees receive a monthly home office stipend:
- **Fully remote:** $150/month
- **Hybrid:** $75/month

This stipend covers internet, electricity, and other incidental expenses.

### 6.3 Ergonomic Equipment

Employees may request ergonomic equipment (standing desk, ergonomic chair) through the IT portal. Requests up to $500 are approved automatically; requests above $500 require manager approval.

## 7. Communication and Availability

### 7.1 Response Times

- **Slack/Teams messages:** Respond within 1 hour during core hours.
- **Email:** Respond within 4 hours during business hours.
- **Urgent matters:** Respond within 15 minutes (marked with [URGENT] tag).

### 7.2 Video Meetings

Employees are expected to have their cameras on during team meetings and 1:1s unless bandwidth issues prevent it. A professional or neutral background (physical or virtual) is required.

### 7.3 Status Updates

Remote employees must keep their Slack/Teams status updated to reflect their availability (Available, In a Meeting, Away, Lunch, etc.).

## 8. Performance and Accountability

Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as in-office employees. Managers will:
- Conduct weekly 1:1 check-ins.
- Set clear, measurable goals and deliverables.
- Use project management tools (Jira, Asana) to track progress.
- Conduct quarterly performance reviews.

## 9. Security Requirements

Remote employees must:
- Use the company VPN for all work-related access.
- Enable full-disk encryption on all work devices.
- Lock their workstation when stepping away.
- Not use public Wi-Fi for work without VPN.
- Follow the Information Security Policy (POL-005).

## 10. Revocation

The company reserves the right to revoke remote work privileges if:
- Performance declines below acceptable standards.
- The employee violates security protocols.
- Business needs require in-office presence.
- The employee fails to maintain adequate communication.

Written notice of 2 weeks will be provided before revocation, except in cases of security violations.

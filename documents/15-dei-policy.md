# Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Policy

**Document ID:** POL-015  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corporation is committed to fostering a diverse, equitable, and inclusive workplace where all employees feel valued, respected, and empowered to contribute their best work. This policy outlines our commitment and the programs we have in place to support DEI.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all aspects of employment at Acme Corporation, including recruiting, hiring, onboarding, compensation, promotions, training, benefits, and workplace culture.

## 3. Our Commitments

### 3.1 Diversity

We actively seek to build a workforce that reflects the diversity of the communities we serve. This includes diversity of race, ethnicity, gender, age, sexual orientation, disability, veteran status, religion, national origin, and thought.

### 3.2 Equity

We are committed to fair treatment, access, and opportunity for all employees. We identify and eliminate barriers that prevent full participation of all groups.

### 3.3 Inclusion

We cultivate a culture where all employees feel welcome, heard, and able to contribute fully. We value different perspectives and create spaces for all voices.

## 4. Recruitment and Hiring

### 4.1 Inclusive Job Postings

- All job postings are reviewed for inclusive language.
- Job requirements focus on essential skills and avoid unnecessary barriers.
- Postings are distributed across diverse job boards and platforms.

### 4.2 Diverse Candidate Pools

- Hiring managers must ensure diverse candidate slates (the Rooney Rule: at least one candidate from an underrepresented group for every open position).
- Partnerships with diversity-focused organizations and universities.
- Blind resume screening is used for initial candidate evaluation.

### 4.3 Interview Process

- Structured interviews with standardized questions and scoring rubrics.
- Diverse interview panels (minimum 2 interviewers from different backgrounds).
- Interviewers must complete Unconscious Bias Training before participating in interviews.

## 5. Pay Equity

### 5.1 Compensation Reviews

- Annual pay equity analysis conducted by HR and an external consultant.
- Pay adjustments are made to address any identified disparities.
- Salary bands are transparent and published internally.

### 5.2 Salary Transparency

- All job postings include salary ranges.
- Employees may discuss compensation openly (protected right).
- Promotion and raise criteria are documented and accessible.

## 6. Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)

Acme Corporation supports the following Employee Resource Groups:

| ERG Name                | Focus Area                      |
|------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Women@Acme             | Gender equity and women's advancement |
| Pride@Acme             | LGBTQ+ community and allies    |
| Mosaic@Acme            | Racial and ethnic diversity     |
| Veterans@Acme          | Military veterans and families  |
| Ability@Acme           | Disability inclusion            |
| Parents@Acme           | Working parents and caregivers  |
| NextGen@Acme           | Early career professionals      |

### 6.1 ERG Support

- Each ERG receives an annual budget of $5,000 for events and programming.
- ERG leaders receive 2 hours per week of paid time for ERG activities.
- ERGs have executive sponsors from the leadership team.
- ERG activities count toward performance review goals.

## 7. Accessibility

### 7.1 Physical Accessibility

- All office locations comply with ADA requirements.
- Accessible parking, entrances, restrooms, and workspaces are available.
- Service animals are welcome in all company facilities.

### 7.2 Digital Accessibility

- All company software and web applications must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
- Documents and presentations should be created with accessibility in mind.
- Captions are provided for all company videos and webinars.

### 7.3 Accommodations

Employees may request reasonable accommodations through HR. The interactive accommodation process includes:
1. Employee submits accommodation request.
2. HR engages in an interactive dialogue with the employee.
3. Medical documentation may be requested (kept confidential).
4. Accommodation is implemented within 10 business days when feasible.
5. Accommodations are reviewed annually or upon change of role.

## 8. Training and Education

- **Unconscious Bias Training:** Mandatory for all employees annually.
- **Inclusive Leadership:** Required for all people managers.
- **Allyship Workshops:** Offered quarterly (optional but encouraged).
- **Cultural Competency:** Available through the Learning & Development portal.
- **Pronoun and Gender Identity Awareness:** Part of onboarding training.

## 9. Reporting and Metrics

### 9.1 DEI Dashboard

The company publishes a quarterly DEI dashboard (available on the intranet) that includes:
- Workforce demographic data (anonymized and aggregated).
- Hiring and promotion rates by demographic group.
- Pay equity metrics.
- Employee engagement scores by demographic group.
- ERG participation rates.

### 9.2 Annual DEI Report

An annual DEI report is published to all employees and the board of directors, summarizing progress, challenges, and goals for the coming year.

## 10. Accountability

- The Chief People Officer is the executive sponsor for DEI initiatives.
- Each department has a designated DEI Champion.
- DEI goals are included in leadership performance reviews.
- The company conducts an annual DEI climate survey.

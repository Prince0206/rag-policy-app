# Code of Conduct and Ethics Policy

**Document ID:** POL-006  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Legal / Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards and professional behavior expected of all Acme Corporation employees. It serves as a guide for making decisions that reflect our values of integrity, respect, and accountability.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, officers, directors, and anyone acting on behalf of Acme Corporation, including contractors and consultants.

## 3. Core Values

### 3.1 Integrity

We act honestly and ethically in all our business dealings. We do what is right, even when no one is watching.

### 3.2 Respect

We treat all individuals with dignity and respect, regardless of their position, background, race, gender, age, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or any other characteristic.

### 3.3 Accountability

We take responsibility for our actions and decisions. We follow through on commitments and own our mistakes.

### 3.4 Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we do. We continuously improve and innovate to deliver the best results.

## 4. Workplace Behavior

### 4.1 Professional Conduct

Employees are expected to:
- Maintain a professional demeanor in all workplace interactions.
- Communicate respectfully in person, via email, chat, and during meetings.
- Be punctual and prepared for meetings and commitments.
- Dress appropriately for the work environment (see Dress Code section).

### 4.2 Dress Code

- **Office days:** Business casual (collared shirts, slacks, skirts, dresses; no torn jeans, tank tops, or flip-flops).
- **Client meetings:** Business professional.
- **Remote days:** Professional appearance from waist up for video calls.
- **Casual Fridays:** Relaxed casual (jeans, sneakers acceptable; no offensive graphics).

### 4.3 Substance Use

- Alcohol consumption is not permitted during work hours or on company premises, except at company-sanctioned events.
- Use of illegal drugs or controlled substances is strictly prohibited.
- Employees must not be under the influence of alcohol or drugs while performing work duties.
- Prescription medication that may impair job performance should be disclosed to HR confidentially.

## 5. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

### 5.1 Zero Tolerance

Acme Corporation has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, and bullying. This includes but is not limited to:
- Sexual harassment (unwelcome advances, requests for sexual favors, verbal or physical conduct)
- Racial or ethnic slurs, jokes, or derogatory comments
- Gender-based harassment or discrimination
- Harassment based on age, disability, religion, or sexual orientation
- Cyberbullying via company communication platforms

### 5.2 Reporting

Employees who experience or witness harassment should:
1. Report to their manager (if appropriate) or skip-level manager.
2. Contact HR directly at hr@acmecorp.com or ext. 2100.
3. Use the anonymous Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS or ethics.acmecorp.com.

All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest

### 6.1 Definition

A conflict of interest exists when an employee's personal interests interfere (or appear to interfere) with the interests of the company.

### 6.2 Examples

- Holding a financial interest in a competitor or vendor.
- Employment or consulting for a competitor.
- Hiring or supervising a family member.
- Accepting gifts or entertainment that could influence business decisions.

### 6.3 Disclosure

All potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the employee's manager and the Legal department. The Legal department will determine whether a conflict exists and prescribe appropriate actions.

## 7. Gifts and Entertainment

### 7.1 Receiving Gifts

- Gifts under $50 in value may be accepted without disclosure.
- Gifts between $50 and $200 must be disclosed to the employee's manager.
- Gifts over $200 must be declined or donated to the company charity fund.
- Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards) must never be accepted.

### 7.2 Giving Gifts

- Business gifts to clients or partners must be reasonable and appropriate.
- Maximum value: $100 per gift per recipient per year.
- All gifts must be reported through the Finance department.
- Gifts to government officials are prohibited without Legal approval.

## 8. Confidentiality

All employees must protect confidential company information, including:
- Trade secrets and intellectual property
- Financial information and business strategies
- Customer and employee personal data
- Proprietary processes and technologies

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends, as specified in the employee's Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement.

## 9. Social Media

### 9.1 Personal Use

- Employees may use personal social media during breaks.
- Employees must not represent personal opinions as company positions.
- Company confidential information must never be shared on social media.

### 9.2 Official Company Accounts

Only authorized Communications team members may post on official company social media accounts. All official posts must be approved by the Communications Manager.

## 10. Compliance and Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Serious violations may also be referred to law enforcement.

Employees are encouraged to speak up when they observe behavior that conflicts with our values. The company is committed to creating a culture where ethical behavior is the norm and misconduct is addressed swiftly.

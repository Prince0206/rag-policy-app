# Intellectual Property Policy

**Document ID:** POL-014  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Legal  

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for the creation, protection, and management of intellectual property (IP) at Acme Corporation. Protecting our IP is essential to maintaining our competitive advantage and business value.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and interns who create, access, or handle intellectual property on behalf of Acme Corporation.

## 3. Definitions

- **Intellectual Property (IP):** Creations of the mind, including inventions, designs, software, documentation, processes, trademarks, and trade secrets.
- **Work Product:** Any IP created by an employee within the scope of their employment or using company resources.
- **Trade Secret:** Confidential business information that provides a competitive advantage (e.g., algorithms, customer lists, formulas, strategies).

## 4. Ownership of Work Product

### 4.1 Company Ownership

All work product created by employees during their employment, using company resources or time, or related to the company's business, is the sole property of Acme Corporation. This includes:
- Software code and documentation
- Inventions and designs
- Reports, presentations, and analyses
- Marketing materials and content
- Databases and datasets

### 4.2 Prior Inventions

Employees must disclose any prior inventions or IP at the time of hiring by completing the Prior Inventions Disclosure Form. Listed prior inventions remain the employee's property.

### 4.3 Personal Projects

Employees may work on personal projects outside of work hours, provided:
- The project does not use company resources (hardware, software, data).
- The project does not compete with or relate to Acme Corporation's business.
- The project does not use any company confidential information.
- The employee has disclosed the project to their manager and Legal.

## 5. Invention Disclosure

### 5.1 Duty to Disclose

Employees must promptly disclose any inventions, discoveries, or innovations that:
- Are made within the scope of employment.
- Relate to the company's current or anticipated business.
- Result from the use of company resources or information.

### 5.2 Disclosure Process

1. Complete an Invention Disclosure Form (available on the Legal intranet page).
2. Submit to the Legal department at ip@acmecorp.com.
3. The IP Review Committee will evaluate the disclosure within 30 days.
4. The committee will determine whether to pursue patent protection, maintain as a trade secret, or release to the employee.

### 5.3 Inventor Recognition

Employees who contribute to patentable inventions receive:
- A $2,000 bonus upon patent filing.
- An additional $3,000 bonus upon patent grant.
- Recognition in the company's Innovation Hall of Fame.

## 6. Confidentiality and Trade Secrets

### 6.1 Protection

All employees must:
- Sign a Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) upon hiring.
- Protect trade secrets using appropriate security measures.
- Share confidential information only on a need-to-know basis.
- Mark confidential documents as "CONFIDENTIAL" or "RESTRICTED."

### 6.2 Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive the termination of employment for a period of 3 years for confidential information and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 7. Third-Party IP

### 7.1 Respect for Others' IP

Employees must:
- Not use third-party copyrighted material without proper licenses.
- Not reverse-engineer competitors' products or software.
- Properly attribute and license any open-source software used in company projects.
- Report any potential IP infringement to the Legal department.

### 7.2 Open Source Software

- Use of open-source software must be approved by the Legal and Engineering teams.
- Permissive licenses (MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0) are generally approved.
- Copyleft licenses (GPL, AGPL) require Legal review before use.
- All open-source dependencies must be documented in the project's dependency manifest.

## 8. Departing Employees

Upon leaving Acme Corporation, employees must:
- Return all company materials, documents, and data.
- Confirm deletion of any company data from personal devices.
- Acknowledge continuing confidentiality obligations.
- Complete an IP exit interview with the Legal department.

## 9. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Legal action to protect the company's IP rights.
- Recovery of damages caused by unauthorized disclosure or use.

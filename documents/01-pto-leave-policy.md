# Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy

**Document ID:** POL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for Paid Time Off (PTO) and various leave types available to all eligible employees of Acme Corporation. The goal is to provide employees with flexible time away from work to rest, handle personal matters, and maintain work-life balance.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for PTO benefits starting from their first day of employment. Part-time employees working 20 or more hours per week receive prorated PTO benefits. Temporary and contract employees are not eligible for PTO.

## 3. PTO Accrual Rates

PTO is accrued on a per-pay-period basis according to the following schedule:

### 3.1 Standard Accrual

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Accrual Per Pay Period |
|-------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| 0 – 2 years      | 15 days         | 4.62 hours           |
| 3 – 5 years      | 20 days         | 6.15 hours           |
| 6 – 10 years     | 25 days         | 7.69 hours           |
| 11+ years        | 30 days         | 9.23 hours           |

### 3.2 Maximum Accrual Cap

Employees may accumulate a maximum of 1.5 times their annual PTO allotment. Once the cap is reached, no additional PTO will accrue until the balance falls below the cap. For example, an employee with 15 annual PTO days can accumulate up to 22.5 days.

## 4. Requesting PTO

### 4.1 Advance Notice

- **1–2 days of PTO:** At least 3 business days' notice required.
- **3–5 days of PTO:** At least 2 weeks' notice required.
- **More than 5 consecutive days:** At least 30 days' notice required.

### 4.2 Approval Process

All PTO requests must be submitted through the HRIS portal (Workday). Managers must respond to PTO requests within 3 business days. Requests are approved based on business needs and team coverage. In cases of scheduling conflicts, seniority will be considered.

### 4.3 Blackout Periods

The company may designate blackout periods during peak business seasons (typically Q4 closing: December 15–31). PTO requests during blackout periods require VP-level approval.

## 5. Sick Leave

### 5.1 Allocation

All full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO. Unused sick days do not carry over to the next year and are not paid out upon separation.

### 5.2 Usage

Sick leave may be used for:
- Personal illness or injury
- Medical, dental, or vision appointments
- Care of an immediate family member who is ill
- Mental health days (up to 3 per year)

### 5.3 Documentation

A doctor's note is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive sick days. The company reserves the right to request medical documentation for suspicious patterns of sick leave usage.

## 6. Parental Leave

### 6.1 Birth Parents

Birth parents are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% of base salary. This leave may begin up to 2 weeks before the expected due date.

### 6.2 Non-Birth Parents

Non-birth parents (including adoptive and foster parents) are entitled to 8 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% of base salary. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement of the child.

### 6.3 Additional Unpaid Leave

Employees may request up to an additional 4 weeks of unpaid parental leave, subject to manager and HR approval.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave as follows:
- **Immediate family** (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 business days
- **Extended family** (grandparent, grandchild, in-law, aunt, uncle): 3 business days
- **Close friend or colleague:** 1 business day

Additional unpaid bereavement leave may be granted upon request.

## 8. Jury Duty

Employees called for jury duty will receive full pay for up to 10 business days per calendar year. Employees must provide a copy of the jury summons to HR and their manager as soon as it is received.

## 9. PTO Payout Upon Separation

Upon voluntary or involuntary separation, employees will be paid out for all accrued but unused PTO at their current rate of pay. Sick leave is not paid out upon separation.

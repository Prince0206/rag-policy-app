# Performance Management and Review Policy

**Document ID:** POL-007  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines Acme Corporation's performance management process, which is designed to support employee growth, align individual goals with company objectives, and ensure fair and consistent evaluation of employee performance.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period.

## 3. Performance Review Cycle

### 3.1 Annual Reviews

Formal performance reviews are conducted annually in Q1 (January 15 – February 28) for the prior calendar year's performance.

### 3.2 Mid-Year Check-ins

A formal mid-year check-in is conducted in July to assess progress toward goals and provide developmental feedback. This is not a formal rating but an opportunity for dialogue.

### 3.3 Quarterly Goal Updates

Employees update their goals and progress quarterly in the HRIS system (Workday). Managers review and acknowledge these updates within 5 business days.

## 4. Rating Scale

Performance is evaluated on a 5-point scale:

| Rating | Label              | Description                                                |
|--------|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 5      | Exceptional        | Consistently exceeds expectations in all areas             |
| 4      | Exceeds Expectations | Frequently exceeds expectations in key areas             |
| 3      | Meets Expectations | Consistently meets all job requirements                    |
| 2      | Needs Improvement  | Falls short in one or more key areas                      |
| 1      | Unsatisfactory     | Consistently fails to meet minimum job requirements       |

## 5. Evaluation Criteria

Employees are evaluated in two primary areas:

### 5.1 Goal Achievement (60% weight)

- Completion of individual goals set at the beginning of the review period.
- Quality and impact of work delivered.
- Contribution to team and department objectives.

### 5.2 Core Competencies (40% weight)

- **Communication:** Effectively shares information and ideas.
- **Collaboration:** Works well with others; supports team success.
- **Problem Solving:** Identifies issues and develops effective solutions.
- **Initiative:** Proactively takes on challenges and seeks improvement.
- **Leadership** (for managers): Develops team members; provides clear direction.

## 6. Goal Setting (OKR Framework)

### 6.1 Process

At the beginning of each calendar year, employees work with their managers to set 3–5 goals using the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework.

### 6.2 Requirements

Each goal must be:
- **Specific:** Clearly defined and unambiguous.
- **Measurable:** Include quantifiable key results.
- **Aligned:** Connected to team and company objectives.
- **Time-bound:** Have clear deadlines.

### 6.3 Examples

**Objective:** Improve customer onboarding experience.  
**Key Results:**
1. Reduce onboarding time from 14 days to 7 days by Q3.
2. Achieve 90% customer satisfaction score in onboarding surveys by Q4.
3. Create 5 self-service onboarding guides by Q2.

## 7. Calibration

### 7.1 Process

After managers submit their ratings, calibration sessions are held at the department and division levels. Calibration ensures:
- Consistency in how ratings are applied across teams.
- Fair treatment of all employees regardless of manager.
- Identification of high-potential employees and those needing support.

### 7.2 Participants

Calibration sessions include the department head, all reporting managers, and an HR Business Partner.

## 8. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

### 8.1 Triggers

A PIP may be initiated when an employee:
- Receives a rating of 2 (Needs Improvement) or 1 (Unsatisfactory).
- Consistently fails to meet specific performance objectives.
- Receives multiple documented performance concerns.

### 8.2 PIP Process

1. Manager and HR draft a PIP document outlining:
   - Specific performance deficiencies
   - Expected improvements with measurable targets
   - Resources and support available
   - Timeline (typically 30, 60, or 90 days)
2. Employee meets with manager and HR to discuss the PIP.
3. Weekly check-ins during the PIP period.
4. At the end of the PIP period:
   - **Successful:** PIP is closed; employee returns to regular performance tracking.
   - **Unsuccessful:** Further action, up to and including termination, may be taken.

## 9. Compensation and Reviews

Performance ratings directly influence:
- **Annual merit increases:** Typically announced in March.
- **Bonus eligibility:** Based on individual and company performance.
- **Promotion consideration:** Ratings of 4 or 5 are typically required.

| Rating | Typical Merit Increase | Bonus Multiplier |
|--------|----------------------|------------------|
| 5      | 6 – 8%               | 1.5x             |
| 4      | 4 – 6%               | 1.2x             |
| 3      | 2 – 4%               | 1.0x             |
| 2      | 0 – 2%               | 0.5x             |
| 1      | 0%                   | 0x               |

## 10. Feedback Culture

Acme Corporation encourages continuous feedback beyond formal reviews:
- **360-degree feedback:** Available annually for all employees.
- **Peer recognition:** Use the #kudos Slack channel or the recognition module in Workday.
- **Skip-level meetings:** Employees may request meetings with their manager's manager.
- **Anonymous feedback:** Available through the employee engagement survey (conducted semi-annually).

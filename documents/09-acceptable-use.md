# Acceptable Use of Technology Policy

**Document ID:** POL-009  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Information Technology  

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of Acme Corporation's technology resources, including computers, networks, software, email, internet, and cloud services. The goal is to protect employees, partners, and the company from harm caused by the misuse of IT resources.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all users of Acme Corporation's technology resources, including employees, contractors, consultants, temporary workers, and third-party partners.

## 3. General Use

### 3.1 Company-Owned Equipment

- Company-provided devices are primarily for business use.
- Limited personal use is permitted during breaks and non-work hours, provided it does not:
  - Interfere with productivity
  - Violate any company policy
  - Consume excessive bandwidth or storage
  - Introduce security risks

### 3.2 Software Installation

- Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices.
- Requests for new software must be submitted through the IT Service Desk.
- Unauthorized software will be removed, and repeated violations may result in disciplinary action.
- Approved software list is maintained on the IT intranet portal.

### 3.3 Cloud Services

- Only approved cloud services may be used for company data (see Approved Services list).
- Approved services: Google Workspace, Slack, Zoom, Jira, Confluence, GitHub, Salesforce, Workday, Concur.
- Employees must not store company data on personal cloud accounts (Dropbox, personal Google Drive, iCloud, etc.).

## 4. Internet Use

### 4.1 Acceptable Use

- Research and information gathering for business purposes.
- Professional development and training.
- Accessing company-approved applications and services.
- Limited personal browsing during breaks.

### 4.2 Prohibited Use

- Accessing, downloading, or distributing inappropriate or offensive content.
- Downloading or streaming non-work-related media that consumes excessive bandwidth.
- Accessing gambling, gaming, or adult content websites.
- Using company resources for personal commercial activities or side businesses.
- Circumventing web filters or security controls (e.g., using unauthorized VPNs or proxies).
- Peer-to-peer file sharing (torrenting).

## 5. Email Use

### 5.1 Acceptable Use

- Business communications with colleagues, clients, and partners.
- Sending and receiving work-related attachments.
- Subscribing to work-related newsletters and updates.

### 5.2 Prohibited Use

- Sending mass unsolicited emails (spam).
- Forwarding chain letters or hoax emails.
- Using company email for personal commercial purposes.
- Sending emails that contain harassing, discriminatory, or threatening content.
- Auto-forwarding company email to personal email accounts.

### 5.3 Email Retention

- Business emails are retained for 7 years per the data retention policy.
- Employees should regularly archive or delete non-essential emails to manage mailbox size.
- Maximum mailbox size: 50 GB.

## 6. Messaging and Collaboration Tools

### 6.1 Slack Guidelines

- Use appropriate channels for communication (don't spam #general).
- Keep conversations professional and inclusive.
- Use threads to keep channels organized.
- Do not share confidential or restricted information in public channels.
- Set "Do Not Disturb" outside of work hours to maintain work-life balance.

### 6.2 Video Conferencing (Zoom)

- Use company Zoom accounts for all business meetings.
- Enable waiting rooms for external meetings.
- Do not record meetings without consent of all participants.
- Recordings must be stored in approved locations (not personal devices).

## 7. Password and Authentication

- Follow the password requirements in the Information Security Policy (POL-005).
- Never share passwords with anyone, including IT staff (IT will never ask for your password).
- Use the company password manager (1Password) for storing credentials.
- Enable MFA on all accounts that support it.

## 8. Printing and Physical Resources

- Use double-sided printing by default to reduce waste.
- Minimize color printing to reduce costs.
- Collect printed documents promptly from the printer.
- Shred sensitive documents using the cross-cut shredders located near each printer station.
- Report printer malfunctions to IT via the service desk.

## 9. Monitoring

Acme Corporation reserves the right to monitor the use of all company technology resources, including:
- Internet browsing activity
- Email content and metadata
- File storage and transfers
- Application usage
- Network traffic

Monitoring is conducted to protect company assets and ensure compliance with this policy. Monitoring activities comply with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

## 10. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Warning (first offense for minor violations)
- Temporary suspension of technology privileges
- Formal disciplinary action
- Termination of employment (for serious or repeated violations)
- Legal action (for criminal activity)

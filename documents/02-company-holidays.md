# Company Holidays Policy

**Document ID:** POL-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the paid holidays observed by Acme Corporation. All eligible employees will receive paid time off on these designated holidays.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time and part-time employees (working 20+ hours per week) are eligible for paid holidays. Temporary and contract employees are not eligible unless specified in their contract.

## 3. Observed Holidays

Acme Corporation observes the following paid holidays each calendar year:

| Holiday                        | Date                          |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| New Year's Day                | January 1                     |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day    | Third Monday in January       |
| Presidents' Day               | Third Monday in February      |
| Memorial Day                  | Last Monday in May            |
| Juneteenth                    | June 19                       |
| Independence Day              | July 4                        |
| Labor Day                     | First Monday in September     |
| Indigenous Peoples' Day       | Second Monday in October      |
| Veterans Day                  | November 11                   |
| Thanksgiving Day              | Fourth Thursday in November   |
| Day After Thanksgiving        | Fourth Friday in November     |
| Christmas Eve                 | December 24                   |
| Christmas Day                 | December 25                   |
| New Year's Eve                | December 31                   |

**Total: 14 paid holidays per year**

## 4. Floating Holidays

In addition to the observed holidays, each full-time employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Floating holidays:
- Must be used within the calendar year and do not carry over.
- Must be requested and approved at least 5 business days in advance.
- Are not paid out upon separation.

## 5. Holiday Falling on a Weekend

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, the preceding Friday will be observed. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday will be observed.

## 6. Holiday Pay for Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt employees who are required to work on a designated holiday will receive:
- Regular pay for hours worked, plus
- Holiday premium pay at 1.5 times their regular hourly rate.

## 7. Religious and Cultural Observances

Acme Corporation respects employees' diverse religious and cultural practices. Employees who wish to observe holidays not on the company calendar may:
- Use floating holidays
- Use PTO
- Request an unpaid day off
- Arrange a schedule swap with manager approval

## 8. Office Closure

On observed holidays, all company offices will be closed unless otherwise communicated by the executive team. Essential services personnel may be required to work and will be compensated per Section 6.

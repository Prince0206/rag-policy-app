# Workplace Health and Safety Policy

**Document ID:** POL-010  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised:** January 1, 2025  
**Department:** Facilities / Human Resources  

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corporation is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for all employees, visitors, and contractors. This policy outlines the health and safety requirements and procedures to prevent workplace injuries and illnesses.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, visitors, and temporary workers at all Acme Corporation facilities.

## 3. General Safety Rules

- Employees must follow all safety procedures and instructions.
- Report unsafe conditions or hazards immediately to Facilities or a safety representative.
- Do not operate equipment or machinery without proper training.
- Keep workspaces clean and free of tripping hazards.
- Know the location of emergency exits, fire extinguishers, and first aid kits.
- Do not prop open fire doors or block emergency exits.

## 4. Emergency Procedures

### 4.1 Fire

1. Activate the nearest fire alarm pull station.
2. Evacuate the building using the nearest safe exit.
3. Do not use elevators during a fire.
4. Proceed to your designated assembly point (posted on evacuation maps).
5. Do not re-enter the building until the "All Clear" is given by the Fire Marshal.

### 4.2 Medical Emergency

1. Call 911 (external) or ext. 9111 (internal).
2. Provide first aid if trained to do so.
3. Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.
4. AED (Automated External Defibrillator) units are located near elevators on every floor.
5. Notify HR within 24 hours by filing an incident report.

### 4.3 Severe Weather

- Follow instructions from the building management team.
- Move to interior rooms or designated shelter areas.
- Stay away from windows and exterior walls.
- Remain in shelter until an "All Clear" announcement.

### 4.4 Active Threat

- **Run:** If safe to do so, evacuate immediately.
- **Hide:** If evacuation is not possible, find a secure room, lock/barricade the door, silence phones.
- **Fight:** As a last resort, attempt to incapacitate the threat.
- Call 911 when safe to do so.

## 5. Ergonomics

### 5.1 Office Ergonomics

- All workstations should be set up ergonomically:
  - Monitor at arm's length, top of screen at eye level.
  - Chair adjusted so feet are flat on the floor, thighs parallel to the ground.
  - Keyboard and mouse at elbow height.
  - Take a 5-minute break from screen time every hour.

### 5.2 Ergonomic Assessments

Employees may request a free ergonomic assessment through the Facilities team. Assessments are conducted by a certified ergonomic specialist and may result in recommendations for equipment adjustments or replacement.

## 6. First Aid

- First aid kits are located in every break room and near reception areas.
- At least two employees per floor are trained as first aid responders.
- AED units are inspected monthly by Facilities.
- First aid training is offered quarterly; sign up through the HRIS learning module.

## 7. Workplace Violence Prevention

- Acme Corporation has zero tolerance for workplace violence, including threats, intimidation, and physical aggression.
- All threats of violence must be reported immediately to Security (ext. 9100) and HR.
- Employees with restraining orders should inform HR and Security so that appropriate precautions can be taken.
- Weapons of any kind are prohibited on company premises.

## 8. Environmental Health

### 8.1 Air Quality

- HVAC systems are maintained quarterly to ensure good indoor air quality.
- Temperature is maintained between 68°F and 76°F.
- Employees with sensitivities may request accommodations through HR.

### 8.2 Cleaning

- Common areas are cleaned daily.
- Workstations are cleaned weekly.
- Sanitizing stations are available throughout the office.
- Deep cleaning is performed monthly.

## 9. Reporting and Investigation

### 9.1 Incident Reporting

All workplace injuries, illnesses, near-misses, and safety concerns must be reported:
- **Immediately:** For serious injuries or dangerous conditions.
- **Within 24 hours:** For minor injuries or near-misses.
- **How:** Complete an Incident Report form (available on the intranet or from HR).

### 9.2 Investigation

All reported incidents are investigated by the Safety Committee within 48 hours. Investigation findings and corrective actions are documented and tracked.

### 9.3 Workers' Compensation

Employees who are injured on the job are covered by workers' compensation insurance. HR will guide employees through the claims process.

## 10. Safety Training

- New employee safety orientation is completed during onboarding.
- Annual safety refresher training is mandatory for all employees.
- Role-specific safety training is required for employees in labs, warehouses, or facilities roles.
- Fire drills are conducted semi-annually.
